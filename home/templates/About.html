About Netflix Tudum

Our Story

Netflix Tudum is a hands-on portfolio project built to practice full-stack web development: a Django backend, a real database-backed catalog, and a Netflix-inspired front end with rows, search, and a personal watchlist.

It's intentionally modeled after Netflix's look and feel for learning purposes — it is not Netflix, and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Netflix, Inc. in any way.

What This Project Demonstrates

Beyond the visuals, Netflix Tudum is built to show real, working features rather than static mockups.

  • A database-driven show catalog with genres, cast & ratings
  • Working user registration, login, and logout
  • A client-side "My List" watchlist with instant search
  • Real contact & feedback forms that save to the database
